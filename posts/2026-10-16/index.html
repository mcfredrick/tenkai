Research Worth Reading

  • Expanding Attention — Transforms the exponential attention kernel via truncated Taylor series factorization into exactly n recurrent states updated by rank-one outer products, achieving lossless linear-time attention with fixed memory regardless of sequence length. Theoretically sound route to efficient Transformers.
  • TurboMamba — Hybrid architecture combining a global Mamba block with a local Transformer predictor, augmented by three lightweight lexical memory mechanisms. Claims significantly accelerated inference and training while improving token prediction accuracy.
  • Value-Informed Schedule Trimming — Sparse, value-informed Sequential Monte Carlo guidance that selectively applies reward-based steps during discrete diffusion sampling, reducing compute overhead while maintaining alignment with downstream objectives. Evaluated on discrete image and text generation tasks.
  • Counterfactual Neighborhood Risk Fields — Evidence-grounded framework that constructs risk scores based on counterfactual neighborhood perturbations around VLM representations. Enables selective and trustworthy safety filtering beyond simple harmful/benign separation.
  • L2G-SR — Guides LLM-based symbolic regression with a learned structural verifier alongside numerical error, improving discovery of physically meaningful equations. Reduces spurious surrogate modeling by validating equation structure during search.
  • ARIA — Real-time monitoring and correction system for multi-agent LLM pipelines, preventing error propagation by intervening during execution rather than post-hoc diagnosis. Claims reduced task failure rates on multi-agent benchmarks.
