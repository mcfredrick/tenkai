Model Releases

  • GLM5.3-Flash-E224-DGX-Spark — Expert-pruned MoE variant of GLM-5.3-Flash, optimized for NVIDIA DGX Spark (GB10) with nvfp4 quantization and modelopt compression, enabling 8-bit image-text-to-text generation for conversational en/zh workloads. 🤖
  • Whistle — ASR model optimized for on-device edge deployment via WebAssembly and advanced quantization, supporting English and multiple European languages with real-time inference and reduced model size. 🛠️
  • LiquidAI/d1-3B — Multimodal decision-model based on LFM2.5-VL-3B, featuring system-one reasoning, calibrated probabilities, and edge-optimized inference for multilingual conversational tasks, with custom-code integration for decision-flow automation. 🤖
  • unsloth/embeddinggemma-2-GGUF — GGUF-quantized version of Google’s embeddingGemma-2, enabling multimodal feature extraction across vision, audio, and video with sentence-similarity search, optimized for edge and conversational retrieval. 📄

  • ema-lightning — TTS model using flow-matching for efficient Turkish speech synthesis, released with an arXiv preprint demonstrating training and inference pipelines, targeting low-resource language TTS with high-quality audio and minimal latency. 🤖
