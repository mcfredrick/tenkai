BornSim 0.5.8 — Library implementing vector Born-series scattering models for continuous random dielectric media, enabling accurate simulation of wave propagation in heterogeneous media. Useful for physics-informed ML, synthetic data generation, and inverse problems in electromagnetics.

Research Worth Reading

To Keep or Not to Keep: Learning KV Cache Retention in Disaggregated LLM Serving Systems — Investigates learning-based KV cache retention policies for disaggregated LLM serving systems where prefill and decode are separated across node pools with network fabric. The framework adapts cache admission strategies to minimize cross-node data transfer overhead and breaks classical cache policy approaches.

SimHack: Predicting the onset of detectable reward hacking before RL training — Introduces SimHack, a pre-training analysis framework that predicts detectable reward hacking behavior in LLM code generation before RL training begins. Enabling proactive objective or environment modifications to mitigate obfuscation and training-time degradation.

ReAPO: Retry-Aware Policy Optimization for Efficient Exploration in LLM Reasoning — Proposes ReAPO, a retry-aware policy optimization framework that introduces dependence into rollout sampling by incorporating incorrect solutions into subsequent attempt contexts. The method reduces redundant failed strategy exploration and improves reasoning efficiency under RLVR for LLM agents.

Teaching AI Agent to Play Poker Strategically Through Preference Optimization — Evaluates preference optimization for improving strategic behavior in poker agents under hidden information, demonstrating that PO can surpass static and zero-shot policies in capturing strategic decision-making. The study validates PO as an effective technique for aligning LLM behavior with strategic goals.

Scalable Stress-Constrained Topology Optimization: A Super-resolution and Simultaneous Approach Based on Machine Learning — Presents SNTO, a machine learning framework jointly representing and optimizing state, adjoint, and design variables via custom neural networks for scalable stress-constrained topology optimization. The super-resolution enabled approach significantly reduces computational cost while maintaining structural integrity.

Big Claims, Small Margins: Do Reported GraphLLM Gains Survive Simple Baselines? — Critically evaluates reported SOTA gains of graph LLM methods against simple baselines on text-attributed graph node classification splits from recent studies. Findings reveal that many claimed improvements vanish or shrink substantially with proper baseline comparisons, questioning benchmark rigor.
