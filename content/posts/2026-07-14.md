## 📄⚔️ Research Worth Reading: Tracts from the Enlightenment Salons 🇫🇷

- **[From ML Predictions to Informed Diagnostic Assistance Using the Toulmin Model of Argumentation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.09664)** — 📄🏰 Decomposes image-based diagnosis into claim/grounds/warrant/qualifier/rebuttal/backing for structured, interpretable assessment. Clinicians weary of black-box oracles get a scaffold that shows its work instead of demanding fealty.

- **[Format Sensitivity Index: Token-Controlled Prompt Wrapper Robustness and Schema Compliance in LLM Benchmarking](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.09665)** — 📄⚔️ Quantifies how trivial wrapper formatting flips leaderboard conclusions under a token-controlled protocol, introducing the Format Sensitivity Index. Benchmark oligarchs should note their rankings may be pure typesetting luck.

- **[Faithful, Not Corrective: Message-Format Effects in Multi-Hop Agent Relays Are Tier-Dependent](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.09678)** — 📄🇫🇷 Resolves a dispute in agent literature: structured handoffs help some model tiers and hurt others, while blind format restriction backfires. Relay design gets a nuanced verdict rather than a royal decree from one paper's appendix.

- **[Boltzmann MapReduce: A Partition-Function Reduce for Forkable Sandboxes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.09689)** — 📄🏰 Maps worker confidence densities to Gibbs–Boltzmann measures where inverse temperature equals sample size, yielding a forkable sandbox reduce step. Distributed inference folks get a statistically principled aggregation that needs no central tyrant node.

- **[Interpreting Latent CoT Reasoning as Dynamical Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.09698)** — 📄⚔️ Treats latent reasoning traces (CODI, COCONUT) as dynamical systems with superimposed candidate traces in hidden space, exposing the interpretability gap vs explicit CoT. Peeking behind the curtain of the silent deliberation chamber, one eigenvalue at a time.

- **[YUKTI: From Natural-Language Situations to Robust, Verifiable Decisions...](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.09706)** — 📄🇫🇷 An uncertainty-typed proposition IR with assumption-robust Pareto frontiers and regret certificates, against single-objective NL4Opt-style pipelines. Real-budget allocation gets verifiable guarantees instead of a dictator's point estimate.
