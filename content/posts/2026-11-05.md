## Research Worth Reading

- **[Agent Harness Awareness: Turning Verified Telemetry into Operational Control for Tool-Using Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05391)** — Identifies operational blindness in tool-using agents, where execution harnesses possess real-time health and suitability telemetry unavailable to the model selecting actions. Proposes a framework integrating monitors, traces, and failure histories for health-aware action selection.
- **[A Feasibility Condition for Confidence-Gated Tiered LLM Inference](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05394)** — Derives formal conditions for distributing LLM computation across on-device, edge, and datacenter tiers based on dynamic confidence gating, optimizing the latency-cost-privacy trade-off for frozen model checkpoints.
- **[MA-SafeDiffuser: Safe Multi-Agent Planning with Diffusion Probabilistic Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05524)** — Extends SafeDiffuser to multi-agent settings by embedding control barrier function constraints into reverse-diffusion updates, guaranteeing finite-time safety invariants for joint-agent specifications.
- **[FedSharp: Byzantine Robust Decentralized Federated Learning Under Noisy Channel](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05546)** — Analyzes noise accumulation in decentralized federated learning under Byzantine attacks and noisy channels, demonstrating that significance grows as learning rates decay and enables more effective poisoning. Proposes channel-aware robustness mechanisms.
- **[Towards Robust Long-Lived Agents: Benchmarking Experience Lifecycles in Evolving Environments](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05450)** — Benchmarks how agents discover, reuse, and maintain experience across successive tasks in non-stationary environments, introducing metrics for experience retention and obsolescence.
- **[Objective-Conditioned Active Preference Learning for Multi-Objective Alignment](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05506)** — Proposes active preference learning that objectively selects comparison pairs expected to change labels under multi-objective alignment (helpfulness, harmlessness, honesty), reducing annotation cost while improving alignment precision.

## Today's Synthesis

The practical takeaway is to stop treating agent action selection as a single-model problem. [Agent Harness Awareness](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05391) shows tool-using agents can be operationally blind because the model picking actions lacks real-time harness telemetry. Pair that with [A Feasibility Condition for Confidence-Gated Tiered LLM Inference](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05394) to decide when a local, edge, or datacenter tier should handle a request. An engineer could wire harness monitors, traces, and failure histories into the action-selection loop, then use confidence gating to route low-risk or low-confidence tasks to smaller on-device or edge models while escalating uncertain cases. The concrete build: expose harness health and suitability signals, log execution traces and failures, and gate actions by confidence plus latency, cost, and privacy budgets. Keep frozen checkpoints; use tiered fallback rather than hot-swapping weights ad hoc. This turns operational telemetry from dashboards into control-plane feedback. You would not need the full framework day one, but even basic trace-to-action gating plus confidence-based tiered fallback would test whether the operational blind spot actually degrades tool use.
