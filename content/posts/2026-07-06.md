## Today's Synthesis

The [nationaldesignstudio/rampart](https://huggingface.co/nationaldesignstudio/rampart) PII redaction model, [opik](https://pypi.org/project/opik/2.1.17/), and [deeppolicy](https://pypi.org/project/deeppolicy/0.1.0/) form a powerful privacy-compliance stack for LLM applications. Deploy rampart as a client-side preprocessing layer to sanitize user inputs before they reach your backend, then route the cleaned interactions through opik for trace logging and monitoring, and finally use deeppolicy to evaluate whether the sanitized outputs meet your organization's safety and compliance thresholds. This combination lets you catch PII leakage early while maintaining full audit trails and automated guardrails—all with fully open-source components that you can customize rather than being locked into vendor defaults.
