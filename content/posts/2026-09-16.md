## Research Worth Reading
- **[Optimal Pruning for Neural Architectures using Fisher Information Distances](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16129)** — Parameter pruning via differential-geometric distance in model space; a geometric take on which weights to zero out.
- **[Safe Error Correction for Language Models: Frozen-Base Adjustment with Capability Preservation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16145)** — CRN v2 adds a ~34M parameter logit-level correction module to frozen LMs, fixing errors without degrading base capabilities — practical if you're stuck with a model you can't retrain.
- **[GPEvac: GNN-Based PPO for Adaptive Evacuation Routing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16163)** — GNN + PPO for real-time evacuation routing during shooting events, accounting for adversarial uncertainty and crowding dynamics.
- **[Position: AI Is Not Ready for Strategic Conflicts](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16189)** — Argues LMs are insufficient for high-stakes strategic wargames involving adversaries, escalation, and crisis response; a reality check on simulation hype.
- **[Calibrate, Then Route: Learned Request Routing for Disaggregated LLM Serving](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16206)** — Measured study of routing in disaggregated LLM serving (DistServe, Splitwise, Mooncake), where prefill and decode sit on separate GPU pools.
- **[AI and Biosecurity: Capabilities, Threat Pathways, and Governance](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16213)** — Survey of AI's role in biological research workflows, threat pathways, and defense-in-depth governance frameworks.
