## Model Releases
- **[Ternary-Bonsai-2-27B MLX 2-Bit Quantization](https://huggingface.co/prism-ml/Ternary-Bonsai-2-27B-mlx-2bit)** — A ternary 2-bit Qwen3.8-27B running on MLX with prism Hadamard attention and CUDA/Metal hybrid acceleration. Runs on Apple Silicon with tiny memory footprint, so you can finally do local LLM stuff without a server farm. 🤖
- **[Qwen-Image-2.1 GGUF Quantization](https://huggingface.co/abenzerps/Qwen-Image-2.1-GGUF)** — GGUF-quantized Qwen-Image 2.1 for on-device text-to-image via ComfyUI-GGUF. Cuts VRAM needs so consumer hardware can actually generate images instead of just crashing. 🖼️
- **[Qwen-Image 2.1 Release](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen-Image-2.1)** — Adds text-to-image and editing to Diffusers with RGBA output and multimodal generation. Standard diffusion workflow, but now Qwen can spit out images with alpha channels. 🎨
- **[Qwen3.5 Reranker and Cross-Encoder](https://huggingface.co/AlexWortega/openjev)** — Qwen3.5-based cross-encoder and reranker for NLI and text-classification. Better retrieval in image-text pipelines without training a new backbone. 🔍

## Open Source Releases
- **[AutoClip: AI-powered video highlight extraction and clipping tool](https://github.com/zhouxiaoka/autoclip)** — CV and temporal attention models that auto-detect highlight clips from raw video. Integrates with open-source video LLMs and does frame-level relevance scoring. 🎬

## Research Worth Reading
- **[Elastic Threshold Attention: Learned Contextual Sparsity for Long-Context Decoding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20888)** — ETA adaptively thresholds attention scores per token for learned contextual sparsity during long-context decoding. Reduces KV cache memory-bandwidth bottlenecks by only loading what's necessary. 📉
- **[RBS-Attention: Radius-Bounded Sparse Prefill for Long-Context Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20971)** — Radius-bounded sparse attention to kill the O(n^2) prefill cost in long-context LLMs. Tackles "mean dilution" where block centroids hide relevant tokens in irrelevant noise. 🎯
- **[Detecting Hallucination in LLMs: Tracing the Topological Signatures of Impaired Context Sharing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21096)** — Uses Forman-Ricci curvature of attention graphs to spot structural info bottlenecks and hallucination. Captures semi-local curvature shifts and global topology changes. 🧠
- **[Decoupling Internal Representational Changes and Causal Importance in Fine-Tuned Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21113)** — Probes how fine-tuning messes with internal representations and attention patterns, then decouples representational drift from causal importance via intervention-based attribution. 🔬
- **[TinyCeNN-LM: Quality-Gated Conversion of Pretrained Attention with CeNN-Inspired Cellular-Recurrent Layers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21139)** — Post-training framework replacing pretrained attention with CeNN-inspired cellular-recurrent layers with bounded local processing. Quality-gated routing and fusion to preserve compatibility. 🧬
- **[Can Agents Design Better Chips with a Higher Level Abstraction?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21157)** — Compares four chip design flows using LLM agents: Direct RTL, Agent-based HLS, Post-Compiler HLS Refinement, Post-HLS RTL Refinement. Higher-level abstractions + post-compiler refinement win on speed vs quality trade-off. 🏗️
