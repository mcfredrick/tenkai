## AI Dev Tools

- **[Cline SDK v0.0.85](https://github.com/cline/cline/releases/tag/sdk%2Fsdk%2Fv0.0.85)** — When a model burns through its output token limit without producing usable tool calls, it now retries up to 3 times with appended reminders. Prevents premature run failures from reasoning-heavy responses.
- **[univer: Unified Office Runtime for AI Agents](https://github.com/dream-num/univer)** — Spreadsheets, docs, slides, canvas, relational tables, PDF — all in one runtime. Built as a harness for AI agents doing office-style document manipulation and data processing.
- **[career-ops-hq/career-ops](https://github.com/career-ops-hq/career-ops)** — 72k stars, runs in your local AI coding CLI. Scans job portals, scores listings A-H with a 1-5 global score, tailors your CV, tracks applications. Topics: ai-agent, ai-job-search, ats.
